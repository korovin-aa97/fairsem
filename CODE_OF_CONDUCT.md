# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable in this project.

Assume good faith, critique ideas and code rather than people, and make room
for contributors with different experience levels. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when needed to keep the project safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
